About SalarySeed

Empowering Indian job seekers, graduates, and professionals with transparent, mathematically verified take-home salary calculations.

Our Mission

In India, job offers are almost universally quoted in Cost to Company (CTC) rather than net monthly cash earnings. A fresher offered a "10 LPA package" often expects to receive over ₹80,000 per month, only to discover that their actual credited salary is closer to ₹70,000 after employer PF contributions, gratuity retentions, employee PF deductions, and income tax withholdings.

SalarySeed was built to eliminate this confusion. Our financial utility helps students evaluating campus placements, freshers choosing between multiple offers, and seasoned employees negotiating appraisals to understand the exact breakdown between statutory benefits, taxes, and monthly liquid take-home cash.

Who SalarySeed Serves

  • College Graduates & Campus Recruits: Evaluating entry-level offers (3 LPA, 5 LPA, 7 LPA) to understand their real disposable income.
  • Job Seekers in Transition: Comparing job offers across different corporate compensation structures and metro vs non-metro allowances.
  • Salaried Employees: Determining whether to opt for the New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC) or the Old Tax Regime for annual tax planning.

Limitations of Salary Estimates

While SalarySeed utilizes verified tax brackets under the Income-tax Act, 1961 (Budget 2024 revisions) and statutory EPFO / Gratuity rules, calculations are illustrative estimates:

  • Individual employer compensation policies differ (e.g. food coupons, variable bonuses, medical insurance premium copays).
  • Professional Tax rates vary by state jurisdiction (ranging from ₹0 to ₹2,500/year).
  • SalarySeed provides educational financial guidance and does not replace official chartered accountancy or employer payroll advice.
