10 LPA In-Hand Salary: Monthly Take-Home & Tax Slabs

A 10 LPA CTC offer translates to approximately ₹70,429 per month in your bank account under the revised New Tax Regime (Budget 2024). Explore the detailed component analysis below.

10 LPA Snapshot

  • Annual Take-Home: ₹ 8,45,153
  • Monthly In-Hand: ~₹ 70,429
  • Annual Income Tax: ₹ 37,207 (approx ₹3,101/month TDS)
  • Employee EPF Deduction: ₹ 48,000 (₹4,000/month)

Tax Calculation Details on 10 LPA

Gross salary is ₹9,32,760 (after removing employer PF of ₹48,000 and Gratuity of ₹19,240 from the 10 LPA CTC). Applying the Budget 2024 standard deduction of ₹75,000 brings taxable income to ₹8,57,760:

  • ₹0 to ₹3,00,000: 0% = ₹0
  • ₹3,00,001 to ₹7,00,000: 5% of ₹4,00,000 = ₹20,000
  • ₹7,00,001 to ₹8,57,760: 10% of ₹1,57,760 = ₹15,776
  • Total Base Tax = ₹35,776
  • Health & Education Cess (4%) = ₹1,431
  • Total Annual Tax = ₹37,207
