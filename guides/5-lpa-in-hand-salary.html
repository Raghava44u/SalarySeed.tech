5 LPA In-Hand Salary: Exact Monthly Take-Home & Deductions

Freshers and graduates with a ₹5,00,000 annual package often expect ₹41,666/month. Here is how employer PF, gratuity, and deductions leave you with ₹36,665 per month in hand.

Quick Summary: 5 Lakhs CTC Take-Home

  • Annual In-Hand Pay: ₹ 4,39,980
  • Monthly In-Hand Pay: ~₹ 36,665
  • Income Tax Payable: ₹ 0 (Zero Tax under Section 87A)
  • Total EPF Contribution (Employee + Employer): ₹ 48,000/year

Why is There Zero Income Tax on 5 LPA?

Under the revised New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC), any individual whose net taxable income does not exceed ₹7,00,000 is eligible for a full tax rebate under Section 87A of the Income-tax Act. With a Gross Salary of ₹4,66,380 and the salaried standard deduction of ₹75,000, your taxable income is ₹3,91,380. The computed tax is completely offset by the rebate, meaning ₹0 tax is payable.
