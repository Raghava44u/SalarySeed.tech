CTC vs In-Hand Salary: What Offer Letters Don't Tell You

When evaluating job offers in India, the single biggest surprise for job seekers is the gap between the annual Cost to Company (CTC) figure and the actual monthly amount deposited into their bank account.

The Three Layers of Your Salary

1. Cost to Company (CTC)

Total annual cost your employer incurs to hire and retain you. Includes direct cash pay + retirement benefits + company perks.

2. Gross Cash Salary

Total cash earnings due to you before employee deductions. CTC minus employer PF and gratuity provisions.

3. Net In-Hand Pay

The liquid cash that lands in your bank account on payday after statutory PF, Professional Tax, and Income Tax TDS.

Where Does the Difference Go?

  • Employer EPF (12% of basic): Deposited directly into your provident fund account managed by EPFO.
  • Gratuity (4.81% of basic): Paid only after completing 5 continuous years with the same employer under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.
  • Employee EPF (12% of basic): Deducted from your monthly cash earnings into EPFO.
  • Professional Tax (PT): State levy (₹200/month in states like Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana).
  • TDS (Tax Deducted at Source): Monthly income tax withheld under the New or Old Tax Regime.
