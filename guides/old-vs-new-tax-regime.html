Old vs New Tax Regime: Which is Better for Your Salary?

With the Union Budget 2024 increasing the New Tax Regime standard deduction to ₹75,000 and widening the 5% slab up to ₹7 Lakh, deciding between regimes depends on your total eligible exemptions.

Key Differences at a Glance

Feature New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC) Old Tax Regime
Default Status Default Regime Requires explicit opt-in
Standard Deduction ₹75,000 (Salaried employees) ₹50,000
Zero Tax Threshold Up to ₹7,75,000 Gross (with ₹75k std deduction) Up to ₹5,50,000 Gross
Section 80C Deductions (ELSS, PF, LIC) Not allowed Allowed up to ₹1,50,000
House Rent Allowance (HRA) Exemption Not allowed Allowed under Section 10(13A)
Health Insurance (Section 80D) Not allowed Allowed (up to ₹25k / ₹50k)

The Breakeven Rule of Thumb

For individuals with gross salary between ₹8 Lakh and ₹15 Lakh, the New Tax Regime is generally superior unless your total deductions under the Old Regime (80C + 80D + HRA exemption + home loan interest) exceed ₹3,75,000 to ₹4,00,000 per year.
