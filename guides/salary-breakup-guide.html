Indian Corporate Salary Breakup Guide: Components & Structure

Understanding how HR teams structure your salary components is essential to negotiating compensation, estimating tax liabilities, and maximizing your monthly take-home pay.

Standard Salary Structure in India

Component Typical Ratio in CTC Tax Treatment
Basic Salary 40% – 50% of CTC 100% Taxable. Forms base for EPF & Gratuity.
House Rent Allowance (HRA) 40% – 50% of Basic Exempt under Section 10(13A) in Old Regime; Taxable in New Regime.
Special Allowance Balancing residual figure 100% Fully Taxable.
Employer PF 12% of Basic Salary Included in CTC package; tax exempt up to ₹7.5 Lakh aggregate.
Gratuity 4.81% of Basic Salary Retained by company; tax exempt on receipt up to statutory limits.

Why Higher Basic Salary Decreases Monthly In-Hand Pay

A common paradox in Indian compensation is that a higher basic salary leads to lower immediate monthly cash take-home pay. This occurs because both Employee PF (deducted from your cash pay) and Employer PF (bundled in CTC) are directly tied to 12% of Basic Pay. If Basic Pay is increased from 40% to 50% of CTC, more money is locked away in retirement savings, reducing your monthly liquid cash.
