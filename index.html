Why Your In-Hand Salary is Less Than Your CTC

Companies define CTC as the total cost incurred to employ you. Your bank account only receives the cash salary left after multiple deductions.

1. Employer Contributions

Your CTC includes costs that never appear in your monthly bank transfer, such as the Employer’s 12% PF contribution and Gratuity provisions (4.81% of Basic Salary).

2. Statutory Deductions

Your Employee PF (12% of basic) and State Professional Tax (up to ₹200/month or ₹2,400–₹2,500/year) are deducted at source from your gross monthly salary.

3. Income Tax (TDS)

Tax Deducted at Source is deducted by your payroll team based on whether you opt for the New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC) or the Old Tax Regime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Indian salary structure and take-home calculations.

What is the difference between CTC and In-Hand Salary?
Cost to Company (CTC) is the total amount a company incurs to employ you. It includes deferred benefits like Gratuity and Employer PF. In-hand salary is the actual net cash credited to your bank account after all deductions.
What is the New Tax Regime Standard Deduction for FY 2024-25?
In Union Budget 2024, the Indian government increased the standard deduction for salaried individuals under the New Tax Regime from ₹50,000 to ₹75,000.
