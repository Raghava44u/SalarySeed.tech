Calculation Methodology & Formulas

SalarySeed calculates Indian salaries using exact statutory legislation and tax slabs verified from official government portals.

1. Core Financial Equations

Equation 1 (Cost to Company):

CTC = Gross Salary + Employer PF (12% of Basic) + Gratuity Provision (4.81% of Basic) + Other Employer Costs

Equation 2 (Gross Cash Salary):

Gross Cash Salary = Basic Salary + HRA + Special Allowance + Other Allowances + Bonus

Equation 3 (Net In-Hand Take-Home):

In-Hand Salary = Gross Cash Salary - (Employee PF + Professional Tax + Income Tax / TDS + Other Deductions)

2. Statutory Components Breakdown

  • Employees' Provident Fund (EPF): Under the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, both employee and employer contribute 12% of Basic Salary + Dearness Allowance.
  • Gratuity: Governed by the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972. Annual gratuity liability is calculated as (15 / 26) * (Basic Salary / 12) * 12, equivalent to approximately 4.81% of annual basic pay.
  • Professional Tax (PT): Levied by state governments under Article 276 of the Constitution of India (capped at ₹2,500/year). Our engine applies the standard ₹200/month (₹2,400/year) average across major industrial states.

3. Tax Regimes (Finance Act 2024 Revisions)

SalarySeed implements the revised New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC) introduced in the Union Budget of July 2024:

Income Slab Tax Rate Applicable Conditions
Up to ₹3,00,000 NIL (0%) Basic exemption limit
₹3,00,001 – ₹7,00,000 5% Subject to Section 87A full tax rebate up to ₹7,00,000
₹7,00,001 – ₹10,00,000 10% Marginal relief applies if income marginally exceeds ₹7L
₹10,00,001 – ₹12,00,000 15% Standard slab
₹12,00,001 – ₹15,00,000 20% Standard slab
Above ₹15,00,000 30% Highest standard slab + 4% Health & Education Cess

Standard Deduction: ₹75,000 for salaried employees under New Tax Regime (increased from ₹50,000 in Budget 2024). Under Old Regime, standard deduction remains ₹50,000.
