Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Financial Data Stays in Your Browser

At SalarySeed, your compensation figures are strictly private. All salary values (CTC, basic pay, allowances, deductions, and tax figures) are calculated transiently on your local device via client-side JavaScript.

We do not transmit your salary figures to any backend server, API, remote database, or third-party advertising network. Once you close or refresh your browser tab, your entered salary data is discarded.

2. Authentication Information (Supabase Auth)

Access to our interactive salary calculator and dashboard requires a registered account. Account authentication is handled by Supabase, an open-source cloud authentication platform.

When you register, the following information is stored in the Supabase authentication directory:

  • Your email address
  • A cryptographically hashed representation of your password (your raw password is never stored or visible to us)
  • Account creation and last sign-in timestamps

3. Cookies and Local Storage

SalarySeed uses browser localStorage solely to persist your authentication session token managed by the Supabase client. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or marketing pixels.
